## How to sign

Comment on your first pull request with:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

That comment is recorded against your GitHub account and covers all of your future
contributions. You will not be asked again.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company, or your employment contract assigns your
copyright to an employer, say so in the pull request so a corporate agreement can be sorted
out first. It is far easier to do before the code is merged than after.
